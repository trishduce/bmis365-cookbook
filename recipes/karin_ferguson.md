# Oatmeal Scotchies

**Contributed by:** <Karin Ferguson>
**Serves:** <about 4 dozen>  |  **Time:** <20 mins>

## Why this one

I've made this recipe a few times and they are a deliciuos cookie. I got the recipe off the back of a nestle bag of butterscotch morsels. I highly recomend making these cause they are chewy and delicious. Yum!

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 cup butter
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 3/4 cuppacked brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 3 cups quick or old -fashioned oats
- 1 2/3 cups butterscotch flavored morsels


## Steps

1. preheat oven to 375
2. combine flour, baking soda, sal, and cinnamon in a bowl. 
3. Beat butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract in mixer. 
4. Gradually beat in flour mixture. 
5. Stir in oats and morsels. 
6. Drop by rounded tablespoons onto ungreased baking sheet. 
7. Bake 7 to 8 mins. 
8. Cool on baking sheet for 2 mins. 

## Notes

It's a baking sheet so you should have the right pan. If not go buy on, they are cheap.
