# Easy Homemade Quesadillas

**Contributed by:** Tobin Schye  
**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** 10 minutes

## Why this one

This is a simple recipe I like because it is cheap, quick, and doesn't require many ingredients. It's a good option when I want something filling without spending much time cooking.

## Ingredients

- 1 flour burrito tortilla
- 1 cup shredded cheddar or Mexican cheese
- Optional: cooked chicken
- Optional: salsa or sour cream

## Steps

1. Heat a pan over medium heat.
2. Add cooking oil to the pan.
3. Place one tortilla in the pan.
4. Sprinkle the cheese evenly over the tortilla.
5. Add the second tortilla on top.
6. Cook for about 2 minutes until the bottom is golden brown.
7. Flip carefully and cook for another 2 minutes until the cheese melts.
8. Remove from the pan, cut into pieces, and serve.

## Notes

You can add cooked chicken to make the quesadilla more filling. The quesadilla is ready when both sides are golden brown and the cheese is melted.
