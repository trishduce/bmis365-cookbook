## Why this one

Makes mouth watering pudding cookies

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 package vinila instant pudinding mix
- 1 tsp vinilla 
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/2 cups choclate chips
- 2 1/2 cups flour

## Steps


1. start oven at 350F and prepare cookie sheet with parchment paper
2. In a large bowl, using a handheld mixer, beat the butter until creamy. Add the sugar and continue beating until well combined. Then add the eggs, pudding mix, and vanilla extract and beat until the eggs are well incorporated. 
3.  In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt using a whisk. Add the dry ingredients to the bowl with the wet ingredients and mix until just combined. 
4. Pour in the chocolate chips and stir until they are evenly distributed throughout the dough.
5. Use a small cookie scoop (1 tablespoon size) to scoop out cookie balls on a cookie sheet, placing them 2 inches apart. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes until the bottom of the cookie is lightly browned.
