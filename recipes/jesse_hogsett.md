# Breach and Clear

**Contributed by:** Jesse Hogsett
**Serves:** 1  |  **Time:** 15min

## Why this one

This recipe came to me in a feverous waking dream brought on by sleep deprivation and overwork. It is the optimal pre-game meal for in-person meetings, dates, and standing in line for the checkout at the grocery store. Godspeed and good luck.

## Ingredients

- 1-10 flour tortillas
- 1-2 handfulls shredded cheese of your choice
- 1 Can hot chili
- 1 can refried beans
- salt, pepper, and hot sauce to taste

## Steps

1. Place your first canvas upon a microwave safe surface such as a paper plate, manilla folder, or the microwave's tray (remove from microwave)
2. Apply cheese to the tortilla, leaving ~1/2 inch bare around the tortilla's edge
3. Spoon chili and beans into tortilla to desired filling capacity. Ensure that you can still fold or roll the tortilla
4. Apply seasonings to taste, being careful with the salt
5. Microwave for 20 seconds, roll or fold, then microwave for a further ~40 seconds or until evenly scorching hot througouht the filling
6. Remove from microwave and enjoy responsibly!
