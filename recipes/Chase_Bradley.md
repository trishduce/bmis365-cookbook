# <Sugar Cookies>

**Contributed by:** <Chase Bradley>
**Serves:** Appx. 24 cookies  |  **Time:** 1 hour and 30 minutes

## Why this one

I got this recipe from the internet, it is my favorite type of cookie. These cookies are great for the holidays because they are sweet and work well with icing.

## Ingredients

- 2 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 ½ cups granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ tsp salt
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar (for rolling)

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
3. In a separate large bowl, cream the softened butter and 1 ½ cups sugar together until light and fluffy, approximately 2–3 minutes will do.
4. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until fully combined.
5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until a soft dough forms. Avoid excessive/overmixing.
6. Cover the dough and refrigerate for approximately 30–45 minutes.
7. Scoop the dough into tablespoon-sized portions and roll each into a ball.
8. Roll each dough ball in the remaining 3 tablespoons of sugar.
9. Place the dough balls approximately 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
10. Bake for 9–11 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the centers look slightly soft.
11. Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack.

## Notes

- Use softened butter.
- If you don't have parchment paper, get a baking sheet and lightly grease the baking sheet.
- Salted butter can be used, just don't use the additional salt.
