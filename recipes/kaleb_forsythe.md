# <Chicken and Rice>

**Contributed by:** <Kaleb Forsythe>
**Serves:** <4>  |  **Time:** <15>

## Why this one

I got this recipe from a website called Campbells.com for their recipe on 15-minute chicken and rice dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 1 tbsp. Vegetable oil
- 1 can of Campbell's condensed Cream of Chicken Soup
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/4 tsp. paprika
- 2 cups uncooked instant white rice
- 2 cups fresh or frozen broccoli florets


## Steps

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.  Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat.

2. Add the chicken and cook for 6 minutes, turning once or until golden on both sides. Remove the chicken from the skillet.

3. Stir the Campbell's® Condensed Cream of Chicken Soup, water and paprika in the skillet and heat to a boil.  Stir in the rice and broccoli.

4. Reduce the heat to low.  Return the chicken along with any juices to the skillet.  Sprinkle the chicken with additional paprika.

5. Cover and cook for 5 minutes or until the chicken is no longer pink in the center (internal temperature should reach 165°F) and the rice is tender.  Season to taste before serving.

## Notes

If you don't have campbell's condensed cream of chicken soup, add other cream of chicken soup substitutes.
