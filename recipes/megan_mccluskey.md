## Why this one

I got this from Tastes Better from Scratch. I usually make it for peoples birthday's. I think its worth someone else's time becuase its an easy dessert that just requires layering.

## Ingredients

• 6 large egg yolks
• 3/4 cup sugar
• 1 1/4 cups mascarpone cheese, at room temperature
• 1 1/2 cups heavy cream, whipped to stiff peaks
• 1 cup cold espresso
• 1/2 cup Marsala wine (or rum/coffee liqueur, optional)
• 24 crisp Savoiardi ladyfingers
• Unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting

## Steps

1. Whisk egg yolks and sugar in a heatproof bowl over simmering water (without touching the water) until thick and pale yellow.
2. Cool briefly, then whisk in the mascarpone until smooth. Gently fold in the whipped heavy cream.
3. Combine cooled espresso and Marsala in a shallow dish.
4. Quickly dip ladyfingers for 1–2 seconds so they don't fall apart, then arrange half in a 9-inch pan.
5. Spread half the cream mixture on top. Repeat with the remaining ladyfingers and cream.
6. Chill for at least 4 hours (or overnight) and dust with cocoa powder before serving.

## Notes

Common Substitutions

• Mascarpone: Mix 8 oz softened cream cheese with 1/4 cup heavy cream and 2 tablespoons softened butter until completely smooth. It will be slightly tangier, but the texture holds up well.
• Ladyfingers (Savoiardi): Use sliced pound cake, sponge cake, or butter biscotti. Just adjust your dipping time—dense pound cake needs barely a brush of coffee, while hard biscotti needs an extra second or two.
