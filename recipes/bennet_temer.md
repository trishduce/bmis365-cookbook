# <Peanut Butter and Honey Toast>

**Contributed by:** <Bennet Temer>
**Serves:** <n1>  |  **Time:** <2-4 minutes>

## Why this one

I like to eat peanut butter and honey toast and I eat it frequently.

## Ingredients

- Roughly Two tbsp Peanut Butter
- Honey to Taste
- One Slice of any Bread of your Choice

## Steps

1. Toast Bread to your Desired Crispness
2. Spread Peanut Butter on Toast
3. Drizzle Honey on Top

## Notes

If you don't have honey you can substitute jelly.
