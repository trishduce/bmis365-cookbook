# Scratch Mac & Cheese

**Contributed by:** Teddy Higgins
**Serves:** 2-5  |  **Time:** 30 minutes

## Why this one

My dad has this recipe and its a super easy and delicious Man & Cheese from scrach making your own rue

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp flour
- 3-4 cups of milk (depending on thickness)
- 2 cups of freshly shredded sharp cheddar
- 2 cups of freshly shredded sharp white cheddar
- good helping of whatever freshley shredded cheese you desire
- salt and pepper to taste
- half a box of macoroni noodles

## Steps

1. Begin by bringing a large to medium pot to a boil. Once boiling add your noodles with a pinch of salt. Cook to disired time on box.
2. Then starting melting 1tbsp of butter over low heat in a seperate medium size pan. Once melted add the flour and stir to combine.
3. Begin adding the milk into the flour and butter mixture extremely slowly (1/2cup at a time), and stir continuously. Wait for the rue to thicken to a gravy texture before adding more milk.
4. Once all the milk is added slowly add the freshly shredded cheese allowing it to completely melt and combine, leave a small portion of cheese for a topping at the end.
5. Add pepper to taste no salt.
6. Once noodles are done drain and set aside.
7. once plated add the extra cheese as derired on top.

## Notes

You are making a rue, take your time do not rush it, and keep the heat low to not burn it. taking your time on the milk is the most important, feel free to use heavy wipping cream if desired.
