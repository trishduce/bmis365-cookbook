# Cheesy Garlic Bread

**Contributed by:** Briston Flug
**Servings:** 6 | **Time:** 20 minutes

## Why this one

This is a simple side to go with pasta or pizza. It only takes a few ingredients, and the cheese makes it even better.

## Ingredients

- 1 loaf French bread
- 1/2 cup softened butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Cut the bread in half lengthwise and place both halves on a baking sheet with the cut sides facing up.
3. Mix the softened butter, garlic, and Italian seasoning in a small bowl.
4. Spread the butter mixture evenly over both halves of the bread.
5. Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan over the top.
6. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the edges are golden. Give it another minute or two if needed.
7. Let it cool for a couple of minutes, then cut into slices and serve.

## Notes

Use 1 teaspoon of garlic powder if you don't have fresh garlic. Add a little crushed red pepper if you want some heat.
